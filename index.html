How it works
01
Add to your browserChrome / Firefox: drag the FontPeek button to your bookmarks bar. Safari: click "Copy code", create a new bookmark, and paste the code as the URL.
02
Navigate to any other websiteFind a site with typography you want to inspect. The bookmarklet works on any page — not this one.
03
Click "FontPeek" in your bookmarks barThe inspector activates instantly on that page — no reload required.
04
Hover over any text elementThe panel follows your cursor and shows font family, weight, size, line-height, letter-spacing and hex color.
05
Click to lock — then hit "Copy CSS"Clicking any element freezes the panel so you can click "Copy CSS". Click again to unlock. Press Esc to exit.
